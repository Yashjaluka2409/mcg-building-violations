WHEREAS on inspection it has been found that the erection of the building / execution of the work described above has been commenced or is being carried on (but has not been completed) without or contrary to the sanction referred to in section 254 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 / in contravention of the conditions of sanction / the provisions of the Act, the Haryana Building Code, 2017 and the bye-laws;

NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 262(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994, you are hereby ORDERED to STOP the erection of the building / execution of the work FORTHWITH.

If this order is not complied with, the Commissioner may require the police to remove you and all your assistants and workmen from the premises (section 262(2)) and may depute a police officer / Corporation employee to watch the premises at your cost (section 262(3)-(4)). Continuation of work after service of this order is punishable under section 380 of the Act (fine up to Rs 2,000 and Rs 200 per day) and section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and renders the premises liable to sealing under section 263A and demolition under section 261.

हरियाणा नगर निगम अधिनियम, 1994 की धारा 262(1) के अंतर्गत आपको आदेश दिया जाता है कि उपरोक्त निर्माण कार्य तत्काल रोक दें। आदेश की अवहेलना पर धारा 262(2) के अंतर्गत पुलिस के माध्यम से श्रमिकों को हटाया जाएगा, परिसर सील किया जा सकेगा तथा धारा 380 एवं भारतीय न्याय संहिता, 2023 की धारा 223 के अंतर्गत अभियोजन किया जाएगा।
