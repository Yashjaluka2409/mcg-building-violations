WHEREAS on inspection it has been found that you are in unauthorised occupation of land / premises vested in the Municipal Corporation Gurugram{% if case.govt_parcel %} (parcel: {{ case.govt_parcel.name|default(case.govt_parcel.khasra_no) }}, {{ case.govt_parcel.get_agency_display() }}{% if case.govt_parcel.village %}, village {{ case.govt_parcel.village }}{% endif %}){% endif %} and have raised the unauthorised construction described above thereon, in contravention of section 408 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994;

NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers of the competent authority under section 408A(1) read with section 2(4A) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994, you are hereby called upon to appear before the undersigned{% if hearing_local %} on {{ hearing_local|date("d-m-Y") }} at {{ hearing_local|date("H:i") }}{% endif %} and show cause within {{ notice.response_days }} days from the date of service of this notice (on or before {{ response_due_local|date("d-m-Y") }}) why you should not be ordered to vacate the said premises / land or building / structure constructed thereon, or to demolish the unauthorised construction and restore the land to its original state, or to bring it in conformity with the provisions of the Act.

On failure to show cause to the satisfaction of the undersigned, or to appear, an order under section 408A(2) will be passed requiring you to vacate / demolish / restore within a further period of seven days, whereafter the competent authority shall evict you and take possession / demolish the unauthorised construction under section 408A(3) using such force as may be necessary, and the cost incurred shall be recoverable from you as arrears of land revenue.

हरियाणा नगर निगम अधिनियम, 1994 की धारा 408A(1) के अंतर्गत आपको निर्देश दिया जाता है कि इस नोटिस की तामील से {{ notice.response_days }} दिनों के भीतर ({{ response_due_local|date("d-m-Y") }} तक) अधोहस्ताक्षरी के समक्ष उपस्थित होकर कारण बताएँ कि आपको उक्त निगम भूमि/परिसर खाली करने अथवा अनधिकृत निर्माण ध्वस्त कर भूमि को मूल स्थिति में लाने का आदेश क्यों न दिया जाए। असफल रहने पर धारा 408A(2) एवं (3) के अंतर्गत कार्रवाई की जाएगी और लागत भू-राजस्व के बकाया के रूप में वसूल की जाएगी।

Service: by person / by post / by affixation on a conspicuous part of the premises / by beat of drum as provided in section 408A(1). Appeal against the order under section 408A(2) lies to the Commissioner within seven days (section 408B).
